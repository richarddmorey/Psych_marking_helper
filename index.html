How to use this tool

Enter your name in the top bar, choose the template, add text to the boxes under each section, then click the red Copy tab on the left edge. A formatted version of everything you have written is copied, ready to paste into the text feedback box in TurnItIn.

It is not expected that you will comment on every section, particularly if the submission is very good. To keep the process efficient, salient comments about mechanisms for improving can be reserved for the final section.

  • If you fill in only one box in a section, no prefix is added — useful when your comment does not fit the two-box pattern.
  • If you fill in both boxes in a section, the two comments are separated by a blank line in the copied output, so the strengths and the improvements read as distinct paragraphs.
  • Sections you leave completely blank are omitted from the copied text, so you can skip any section you have nothing to say about.
  • Your marker name is required and is added at the top of the copied text, so the feedback is attributed to you.
  • Write your feedback in paragraphs — pressing Enter inside a box starts a new line, and your paragraph breaks are preserved when the feedback is pasted.
  • Click a section heading to collapse or expand it; use the × in its corner to clear just that section.
  • The Timer button in the top-left corner of the page opens a panel containing both the timer and its settings. Click it again to hide the panel. Start / Stop runs and pauses the timer; Reset zeroes it back to 0:00.
  • The same panel configures two optional cues:
    • Warning — two tones a second apart when the timer reaches your chosen time.
    • Alarm — a short triple beep repeated every 1.5 seconds for about nine seconds.
    • When either cue fires, the timer panel slides open for three seconds if it was closed, so you can see where you are.
  • Set Warning and Alarm times as minutes and seconds. Invalid times (0:00, negative, or empty) are highlighted and the toggle is switched off automatically. Timer settings are saved on this device only.
  • Your work is auto-saved per template on this device. If a template changes, drafts tied to that template are cleared. If you return after a long gap, older drafts are not restored. Switch templates to keep each draft intact in the meantime.
  • The word and character counts in the top bar total the text across every feedback box and update as you type.
  • Pull out the Preview tab on the left edge to see a rendered view of your feedback before copying. Clicking Copy never opens the preview. Press Ctrl/Cmd + Enter anywhere to copy.
  • The Preview panel includes an “Include HTML tags” checkbox. Leave it ticked to copy text with HTML tags; untick it to copy plain text with real line breaks instead, for places that don't accept HTML.
  • The copied HTML text contains tags such as <br> and <b>…</b>. A plain-text editor will show them literally; a rich-text box may render them.
  • Each newline you type is encoded as a <br> tag in the HTML output — this is how your paragraph breaks survive the paste.
